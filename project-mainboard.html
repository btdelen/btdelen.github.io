3D Printer & CNC Mainboard

KiCad PCB Layout Stepper Control Hardware Design

Overview

A full-featured custom mainboard designed from scratch in KiCad for 3D printers and CNC/PCB routers. It combines stepper motor control, high-current bed/hotend heating, and multi-axis limit switch management into a single robust PCB.

Key Features

  • 🔌
    Stepper Motor Control

    Precision driving for X, Y, Z, and Extruder axes

  • ⚡
    Robust Power Delivery

    High current handling for heated beds and tool heads

  • 🚨
    Limit & Emergency Switches

    Integrated pull-ups and hardware debouncing on limits

  • 📏
    Custom Layout

    Layer-optimized signal routing with KiCad
